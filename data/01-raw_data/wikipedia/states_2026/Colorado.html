The 2026 Colorado gubernatorial election will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect the governor of Colorado. Incumbent Democratic governor Jared Polis is ineligible to seek re-election to a third consecutive term. Primary elections took place on June 30, 2026.[1]

In the Democratic primary, Colorado Attorney General Phil Weiser defeated U.S. senator Michael Bennet. If elected, Weiser would be the second consecutive Jewish governor after Polis. The Republican Party has not won a gubernatorial election in Colorado since 2002 nor a statewide office since 2016.[2] Republican nominee Victor Marx's candidacy has been mired in controversy; Marx has made a number of dubious claims and has notably stated he is unsure how many people he has killed.[3] Leading to the Republican primary, both of Marx's opponents indicated they would refuse to endorse him if he won the nomination.[4]

Democratic primary

Candidates

To move forward to the primary, candidates had to petition onto the ballot or achieve 30% or more at the Colorado Democratic Party State Assembly. Michael Bennet was the only candidate to petition onto the primary ballot. All other candidates went through the State Assembly process. Phil Weiser was the only candidate to achieve 30% or more.[5] Bennet's petition was deemed sufficient on March 24, 2026.[6]

Nominee

  • Phil Weiser, attorney general of Colorado (2019–present)[7]
    • Running mate: Lesley Dahlkemper, Jefferson County commissioner[8]

Eliminated in primary

  • Michael Bennet, U.S. senator (2009–present)[9]

Declined

  • Jason Crow, U.S. representative for Colorado's 6th congressional district (2019–present) (running for re-election; endorsed Bennet)[10][11]
  • Jena Griswold, secretary of state of Colorado (2019–present) (running for attorney general)[12]
  • Mike Johnston, mayor of Denver (2023–present) and candidate for governor in 2018[13] (endorsed Bennet)[9]
  • Joe Neguse, U.S. representative for Colorado's 2nd congressional district (2019–present) and nominee for secretary of state in 2014 (running for re-election; endorsed Bennet)[10][11]

Republican primary

Candidates

To move forward to the primary, candidates had to petition onto the ballot or achieve 30% or more at the Colorado Republican Party State Assembly. Barbara Kirkmeyer was the only candidate to petition onto the primary ballot. Her petition was deemed sufficient on April 15, 2026.[36] All other candidates went through the State Assembly process. Scott Bottoms and Victor Marx were the only candidates to achieve 30% or more.[37]

Nominee

  • Victor Marx, ministry leader[38]
    • Running mate: George Markert, AI strategic advisor[39]

Eliminated in primary

  • Scott Bottoms, state representative from the 15th district (2023–present)[40]
  • Barbara Kirkmeyer, state senator from the 23rd district (2021–present), candidate for Colorado's 4th congressional district in 2014, and nominee for Colorado's 8th congressional district in 2022[41][37]

Eliminated at convention

  • Jason Clark, money manager, candidate for governor in 2014 and independent candidate in 2010[42]
  • Joshua Griffin, research and development firm founder and candidate for Colorado's 5th congressional district in 2024[38]
  • Will McBride, attorney[38]
  • Jason Mikesell, Teller County sheriff[43]

Withdrawn

  • Mark Baisley, state senator from the 4th district (2023–present) (running for U.S. senate)[44]
  • Greg Lopez, former U.S. representative from Colorado's 4th congressional district (2024–2025) and candidate for governor in 2018 and 2022 (running as an Independent)[44]
  • Joe Oltmann, podcaster[45]
