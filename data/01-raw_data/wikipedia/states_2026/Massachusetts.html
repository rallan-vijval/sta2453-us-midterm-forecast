The 2026 Massachusetts gubernatorial election is scheduled to take place on November 3, 2026, to elect the governor of Massachusetts. Democratic incumbent Maura Healey is seeking a second term. She faces Republican Michael Minogue in the general election. It is one of 39 gubernatorial elections occurring nationwide in 2026, and one of 18 in which the incumbent governor is seeking another term.[2]

Primary elections took place on September 1, 2026, where incumbent governor Maura Healey won the Democratic nomination running unopposed, and Mike Minogue won the Republican nomination against Brian Shortsleeve.[3][4]

In the run-up to the general election, Republican candidate Mike Minogue spent $6.1 million in September 2026 on his election bid, making it the most expensive gubernatorial race in Massachusetts history. Minogue outspent the 2006 Republican nominee and former Lieutenant Governor Kerry Healey, who spent $4.4 million in the run-up to the general election versus Deval Patrick.[5]

Shawn Oliver arrest

Around 11:40 p.m. EST on October 17, 2026, Minogue's running mate Shawn Oliver crashed into multiple parked cars after his SUV rolled over on Dartmouth Street in New Bedford. He was taken to St. Luke's Hospital and subsequently arrested on charges of driving under the influence, negligent operation of a motor vehicle, a lane violation, and speeding. Minogue said he was "extremely disappointed" and asked Oliver to suspend his campaign, saying that he would immediately ask him to resign if elected.[1] Oliver suspended his campaign on October 18, but his name will remain on the ballot.[64]
