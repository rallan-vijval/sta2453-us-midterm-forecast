The 2026 Oklahoma gubernatorial election will take place on November 3, 2026, to elect the next governor of Oklahoma. Republican former state senator Mike Mazzei and Democratic state representative Cyndi Munson are the nominees for their respective parties. Republican incumbent Kevin Stitt is term-limited and ineligible to seek a third term.

Primary elections were held on June 16, 2026, and in races where no candidate receives over 50% of the vote, runoff elections took place on August 25, 2026. Democrats have not won a gubernatorial election in Oklahoma since 2006.

Republican primary

Campaign

In August 2023, KOSU noted that Attorney General Gentner Drummond had raised significantly more than other statewide elected officials in 2023, fueling rumors of a gubernatorial run.[1] The same month, The Black Wall Street Times reported State Superintendent of Public Instruction Ryan Walters was preparing for a 2026 gubernatorial campaign.[2] U.S. Representative Kevin Hern was the first major contender to public acknowledge he was considering a campaign in August 2024, but ruled out a run that December after House Republicans won a narrow majority in the 2024 elections.[3][4] In early January 2025, former state senator Mike Mazzei posted a gubernatorial campaign logo to Facebook.[5]

On January 13, 2025, Drummond announced his campaign for governor. He was the first major candidate to enter the race and the second overall after Leisa Mitchell Haynes.[6] On February 18, former Oklahoma House Speaker Charles McCall officially announced his campaign.[7] Mazzei officially launched his campaign on April 3.[8] In May, former state senator Jake Merrick entered the race followed by businessman Kenneth Sturgell.[9][10] On October 1, former Governor Frank Keating's son, Chip Keating, entered the race.[11]

  • Calup Anthony Taylor[13]

Declined
