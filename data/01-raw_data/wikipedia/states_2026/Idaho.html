The 2026 Idaho gubernatorial election will be held on November 3, 2026, to elect the governor of Idaho. Republican incumbent Brad Little is seeking a third term. He is being challenged by Democratic attorney Terri Pickens.

Primary elections took place on May 19, 2026.[1]

Republican primary

[edit]

Candidates

[edit]

Nominee

[edit]
  • Brad Little, incumbent governor (2019–present)[2]

Eliminated in primary

[edit]
  • Sean Calvert Crystal, candidate for Idaho's 32nd legislative district in 2024[3]
  • Mark Fitzpatrick, former police officer and owner of Old State Saloon[3]
  • Daniel Fowler[3]
  • Ethan Giles[3]
  • Ron James, Teton County commissioner[3]
  • Lisa Marie, perennial candidate[3]
  • Justin Plante, mechanic[3]

  • Idaho Education Association (endorsement rescinded)[5]

  • Professional Firefighters of Idaho[5]
