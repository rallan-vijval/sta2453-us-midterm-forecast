# Governors 2026, by synthetic voters

A forecast of the 39 governor races on 3 November 2026 (36 states and three territories), built from **synthetic survey responses**: large language models answering an election survey while playing real respondents to the Cooperative Election Study (CES). The synthetic answers are combined with past results and published polls, and the election is simulated 10,000 times.
